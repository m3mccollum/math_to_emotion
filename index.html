About this visualization

What it shows

Two single VOID SLOTS sessions on a $20 starting bankroll, simulated with the verified game math, displayed as two-track timelines. The protagonist of each is the INVERT progress bar — the bonus mechanic that converts dry spells into anticipation, surfaced visually as the shaded purple regions on every track when the bar fills and INVERT activates. Two tracks share a common spin-index axis: bankroll over time (with win and near-miss markers) and three emotional channels — Anticipation, Tension, Elation — computed from the per-spin log via a stated psychological model with disclosed coefficients.

The emotional model

Each channel is a sum of named drivers, each scaled by a coefficient from EmotionConfig in emotional_model.py. Channels are clamped to [0, 1].

Anticipation

A = 0.7 × (bar_fill / 12) + near_miss_residual
bar_fill_weight = 0.7 Bar at 12/12 contributes 0.70. The bar is the dominant driver — the mechanic that converts dry spells into anticipation.
near_miss_bump = +0.15 Each 2-of-3-stars near-miss adds a temporary spike on top of the bar-fill base.
near_miss_decay = ×0.7 / spin Near-miss residual decays over roughly three subsequent spins.
INVERT consumes anticipation Hard reset on activation; bar drops to 0, residual cleared.

Tension

T = 0.5 × min(dry / 15, 1) + 0.4 × max(0, 1 − bankroll / start)
dry_spell_weight = 0.5 Maximum contribution from a long losing streak.
dry_spell_saturation = 15 spins The dry-spell driver caps at 15 (close to the Schilling-expected longest losing run at this hit frequency).
bankroll_dip_weight = 0.4 Maximum contribution from bankroll falling below the session start; reaches 0.4 at $0.
pure state function No history-dependent decay; both inputs drop on a win, so relief is automatic.

Elation

E = 0.6 × E_prev + 0.2 × log₂(1 + payout / bet) [+ 0.3 if INVERT]
elation_log_coeff = 0.2 Win spike scaled by log₂(1 + payout/bet). $1.50 → 0.26; $5 → 0.52; $50 → 1.00 (capped).
elation_invert_bump = +0.3 Additive on INVERT activation regardless of payout — the visual transformation itself has affect.
elation_decay = ×0.6 / spin Elation roughly halves every 1.4 spins.
Calibration roadmap

These coefficients are hand-picked for legibility on the two examples shown, not derived from any player dataset. The magnitudes are not real measurements. For this kind of visualization to inform real design decisions, the coefficients would need to be calibrated against player data gathered from real player research. Please view this model as a structural sketch of how math could map to feeling, not as a quantitative claim about how it does.

What it omits

Population-level statistics (each is one session, not a distribution), cross-session memory, auto-spin compression, and any individual player’s actual affect. Sub-bet wins (“losses disguised as wins”) aren’t a lever in VOID SLOTS, so they aren’t shown. The emotional model itself is a stated heuristic — see The emotional model and Calibration roadmap above.

What it makes visible that a histogram cannot

A histogram tells you 60% of $20 sessions bust. It cannot tell you which 60% felt cheated and which 60% had a good ride before busting. The bust example shows three INVERT activations that still ended in $0 — peak-end pain a histogram averages away. The cashout example shows the alternative path: same probabilities, different luck, completely different felt experience.
